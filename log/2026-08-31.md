# 2026-08-31

## Ingest + verify — PAI-1/SERPINE1 aging evidence from a YouTube lead

Reviewed the YouTube video “PAI-1 Impacts Telomere Length, Cell Senescence, And Longevity” under [[sops/scraping-youtube-references]]. The video description contained promotions and no scientific references, so its transcript was used only to identify claims. No wiki claim cites the video; each incorporated claim was mapped to and checked against primary literature.

## Pages added and independently verified

- [[pai-1]] — canonical SERPINE1/PAI-1 protein page. The evidence supports a window-sensitive target: partial lifelong deficiency is associated with favorable metabolic, vascular, telomere, and survival phenotypes, whereas complete deficiency causes delayed bleeding and is associated with cardiac fibrosis. Aging-context druggability is tier 1 because TM5614 reached an aging-focused human study, but no controlled peer-reviewed human geroscience efficacy result exists.
- [[studies/khan-2017-serpine1-longevity]] — 177-person founder-cohort study plus extended-pedigree survival analysis (doi:10.1126/sciadv.aao1617). Verified the approximately 10% longer age-adjusted LTL, 28% lower fasting insulin, 0/43 versus 8/127 diabetes comparison, and 85-versus-75-year median survival. The age-associated LTL slope did not differ, and only 56 of 221 relatives with known lifespan had direct or obligate genotype ascertainment.
- [[studies/khoddam-2025-pai1-aortic-aging]] — human cfPWV comparison and mouse perturbation series (doi:10.1172/JCI196714). Verified the adjusted human effect (−1.182 m/s), exact mouse median-survival change (749 to 875 days; 16.8%, reported as 17%), and the boundary that TM5614 restored PWV but not systolic or diastolic pressure to baseline during continued l-NAME exposure. Source-data/reporting inconsistencies are documented on the study page.

## Claims corrected or not incorporated

- “Almost 20%” mouse lifespan extension was replaced with the paper's reported 17% median-survival increase.
- “Nearly 100 carriers, only one diabetes case” was not found in the published cohort; the verified 2017 comparison is 0/43 heterozygotes versus 8/127 noncarriers.
- “Preserved juvenile epigenetic patterns” was not matched to a published primary study and was not incorporated.
- DNAm PAI-1 in GrimAge is a 211-CpG methylation surrogate, not a plasma PAI-1 assay. It was the strongest individual protein-surrogate lifespan signal, but DNAm pack-years and the final GrimAge composite were stronger predictors.
- The proposed direct PAI-1→PD-L1 senescent-cell immune-evasion chain was not incorporated as established aging biology. Direct PAI-1/PD-L1 evidence located was in tumor models, while senescent-cell PD-L1 evidence does not establish PAI-1 as its upstream cause.
- “Perfect replication” across species was rejected: the CRISPR mouse model reproduces the allele and several directional phenotypes, but the human evidence remains one related founder population and the drug-reversal model used l-NAME stress rather than natural aging.
